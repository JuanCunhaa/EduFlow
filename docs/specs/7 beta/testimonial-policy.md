# Testimonial Policy — Ethical Collection & Use

> Status: DRAFT
> Date: 2026-02-12
> Role: Community Lead + Legal Awareness
> Dependency: `beta-program.md`, `feedback-collection.md`

---

## 1. Core Principles

| Principle | Non-Negotiable Rule |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **No fake testimonials. Ever.** | Every quote on the website came from a real user who actually used the product. |
| **No fabricated personas** | No stock photos with made-up names. Every testimonial is a real person or anonymized at their request. |
| **No incentivized 5-star reviews** | Never pay for positive reviews. Never condition rewards on positive feedback. |
| **Attribution is opt-in** | Users choose if their name, title, and photo appear. Anonymous is always an option. |
| **Users can retract** | Any user can request removal of their testimonial at any time. Comply within 48 hours. |
| **No editing meaning** | You may fix typos and trim length. You may not change sentiment, add claims, or alter meaning. |

## 2. What Counts as a "Real Testimonial"

### 2.1 Valid Sources

| Source | Valid? | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Beta exit survey — written voluntarily | ✅ | Primary source |
| Unsolicited message in Discord/Slack | ✅ | Ask permission before using publicly |
| 1:1 call quote (with recording consent) | ✅ | Transcribe and confirm quote with user |
| Reddit/LinkedIn public comment about ExamFlow | ✅ | Already public, but ask before using on your site |
| Email from a user praising the product | ✅ | Ask permission before using |
| Quote you wrote and asked someone to approve | ❌ | Ghost-written quotes are fake even if "approved" |
| AI-generated testimonial | ❌ | Obvious fraud. Never. |
| Quote from a friend who never used the product | ❌ | This is the fastest way to destroy trust |

### 2.2 The "Would It Survive a Screenshot?" Test

Before publishing any testimonial, ask: **If someone asked this person "Did you actually say this?", would they confirm it?**

If there's any doubt: don't publish it.

## 3. How to Collect Testimonials Ethically

### 3.1 Timing

Ask for testimonials when users have experienced enough to form a genuine opinion:

| When | Why |
|------|-----|
| ✅ After ≥ 15 exams taken | They've used the core product meaningfully |
| ✅ After exit survey (Week 5) | They've reflected on the experience |
| ✅ After they pass their cert exam | Highest emotional moment — most impactful quotes |
| ❌ Day 1 of signup | They haven't used anything yet |
| ❌ Immediately after a bug fix | Gratitude bias — not a product testimonial |

### 3.2 Ask Template (Email)

```
Subject: Quick favor — 2 sentences about your ExamFlow experience?

Hi [Name],

Thanks for being part of the beta. Your feedback has been genuinely 
helpful — I shipped [specific fix they reported] because of your input.

If you're willing, I'd love a short testimonial (2-3 sentences) about 
your experience with ExamFlow. It can be about anything — what helped 
your study, what you liked, how it compared to other tools.

Totally optional. No pressure. And you can choose to be anonymous if 
you prefer.

If you'd like to share:
→ Reply to this email with your quote
→ Let me know how you'd like to be attributed:
  [ ] Full name + cert (e.g., "Jane D., CISSP candidate")
  [ ] First name + initial (e.g., "Jane D.")
  [ ] Anonymous ("CISSP candidate")
  [ ] I'd prefer not to provide a testimonial (no hard feelings!)

Thanks again for testing ExamFlow.
[your name]
```

### 3.3 Ask Template (Discord/Slack)

```
Hey [name] — I've seen you hitting some great scores lately! 🎯

Would you be open to sharing a quick 2-3 sentence quote about your 
experience? It'd go on our website (with your permission). Totally 
optional and you can stay anonymous.

Just DM me here or reply if you're up for it.
```

### 3.4 Video Testimonial (Highest Impact)

For users who volunteer for a video call:

1. Schedule a 10-minute Zoom/Meet call
2. Ask 3 questions:
   - "What were you studying for when you found ExamFlow?"
   - "How has it helped your prep?"
   - "What would you tell someone considering using it?"
3. Record (with explicit consent)
4. Send them the clip before publishing: "Here's the 45-second cut — OK to use?"
5. Only publish after written approval

**Never surprise someone with a published video.**

## 4. Testimonial Consent Process

### 4.1 Required Before Publishing

Every testimonial needs documented consent. At minimum:

```
Consent Record:
- User ID / email: ___
- Date consent given: ___
- Consent method: [ ] Email reply [ ] Survey response [ ] DM [ ] Verbal (recorded)
- Attribution level: [ ] Full name [ ] First + initial [ ] Anonymous
- The exact quote approved: "___"
- User agreed to: [ ] Website [ ] Social media [ ] Marketing email [ ] All
```

### 4.2 Storage

Keep consent records in a simple spreadsheet:

```
| User | Email | Quote | Attribution | Consent Date | Method | Where Used |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|-------------|--------|------------|
```

This protects you if anyone questions authenticity.

### 4.3 Retraction Policy

- Any user can request removal at any time
- Respond within 48 hours
- Remove from all channels (website, social, email templates)
- Confirm removal to the user
- No questions asked, no guilt-tripping

## 5. How to Display Testimonials

### 5.1 On the Landing Page

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  "ExamFlow found my weak spots in domain 1 and 4 that     │
│   I didn't even know I had. After 3 weeks of targeted     │
│   practice, I passed CISSP on my first attempt."          │
│                                                            │
│   — Sarah M., CISSP                                       │
│   ★★★★★                                                   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Display rules:**
- Show the full quote (don't cherry-pick single words out of context)
- Attribution matches what the user approved
- Star ratings only if from a structured survey (not made up)
- If anonymous: "CISSP candidate" — don't invent a fake name
- No stock photos. Real photo (with permission) or no photo.

### 5.2 On Social Media

When sharing a testimonial on Twitter/LinkedIn:

```
"After 3 weeks of targeted practice on ExamFlow, I passed 
CISSP on my first attempt." — Sarah M.

[link to ExamFlow]
```

- Tag the user ONLY if they consented to public attribution
- Don't add claims they didn't make ("ExamFlow helped 94% of users pass!" — unless proven, see `pass-rate-measurement.md`)

### 5.3 On G2 / Trustpilot / Capterra

- Never create accounts on behalf of users
- **Do:** Ask happy users to leave a review: "If you have 2 minutes, a review on G2 would mean a lot — here's the link"
- **Don't:** Tell them what to write, offer a reward for positive reviews, or create fake reviews

## 6. Red Lines — What to Never Do

| Action | Why It's Wrong | What to Do Instead |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| Write a quote and ask someone to "approve" it | It's a ghost-written fake. The words aren't theirs. | Ask them to write their own. |
| Use a real name with a fake quote | Identity fraud. Legal and reputation risk. | Only use quotes you have documented consent for. |
| Pay users for positive reviews | Violates FTC guidelines. Destroys trust when discovered. | Offer beta perks equally to all, not conditional on sentiment. |
| Edit "it was okay" into "it was amazing" | Misrepresentation. | Use the quote as-is, or don't use it. |
| Keep a testimonial after user requests removal | Violates their consent. | Remove within 48 hours. |
| Display "94% pass rate" from 3 users who passed | Misleading statistic from tiny sample. | See `pass-rate-measurement.md` for what you can claim. |
| Use AI to generate testimonials | Fraud. Period. | Collect real ones or wait until you have them. |

## 7. If You Have Zero Testimonials at Launch

This is fine. Most early-stage products launch without testimonials.

**What to do instead:**

| Alternative | Example |
|-------------|---------|
| Show real usage metrics | "1,200 practice exams completed" |
| Show product screenshots | Real UI, real data (with test accounts) |
| Link to your build-in-public posts | "See what we're building" → Twitter thread |
| Founder's personal story | "I built this because I struggled studying for CISSP" |
| Show the beta NPS score | "Our beta users rated us 52 NPS" (if it's good) |

**Do not** fill the testimonial section with fake quotes because you feel pressure to have them. An empty section with a CTA ("Join 60+ beta users studying for CISSP") is more trustworthy than 3 stock-photo testimonials.

## 8. FTC / Legal Awareness

### 8.1 For US Marketing (FTC Endorsement Guides)

- Testimonials must reflect **honest opinions** of real users
- If you gave something of value (free Pro access) in exchange for a testimonial, **disclose it**
- Disclose the material connection: "Beta tester who received free access"
- Don't claim "typical" results unless you can prove they're typical

### 8.2 Disclosure Template

When using a beta tester's testimonial, add:

```
* [Name] participated in ExamFlow's free beta program.
```

This is small but legally important. It prevents claims of undisclosed paid endorsement.

### 8.3 For EU Marketing (GDPR)

- Testimonials with real names = personal data
- Get explicit consent for processing (the consent record above covers this)
- Honor deletion requests under GDPR right to erasure
- Don't transfer testimonial data to third parties without consent
